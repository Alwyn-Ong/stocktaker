# Stocktaker User Manual

Stocktaker is a mobile app for doing a physical stock check: scan each item's
barcode at a location, then submit which items were found to a shared master
inventory sheet.

## 1. Starting the app

The first screen (Landing) shows the app logo and a button:

- **Connect Google** (not signed in yet) — tap it and sign in with the Google
  account that has access to the master inventory sheet. This is needed because
  inventory data is read from and written to a shared Google Sheet.
- **Continue** (already signed in) — goes straight to location selection.

If sign-in fails, you'll see a "Connection failed" alert with the reason — check
your internet connection and that you're using the correct Google account.

The gear icon in the top-right opens **Settings** (see section 5).

Pressing the phone's Back button on this screen asks you to confirm before
exiting the app.

## 2. Choosing a location

After connecting, you'll land on **Select Location**:

1. Pick a location from the dropdown.
2. Tap **Open** to load that location's item list.

## 3. Scanning items

Inside a location, you'll see the list of items and their status (done/not
done) plus three buttons at the bottom:

- **Scan** — opens the camera. Point it at a barcode:
  - Green toast (`✓ Item Name`) — item marked as present and queued for
    submission (whether it's the first scan, or a re-scan of an item that's
    marked present but not yet confirmed on the master sheet).
  - Orange toast (`✓ Item Name — belongs in <location>`) — the item belongs to
    a *different* location. It's still recorded here: its "Current Location"
    is updated to where you scanned it, and the scan is queued for submission
    as usual. The toast just tells you it's out of place.
  - Orange toast (`Already scanned`) — item is already confirmed present at
    this location on the master sheet; nothing changes.
  - Red toast (`Unknown barcode`) — the scanned barcode isn't in the master
    list at all (any location). It's recorded separately and will appear in
    the report's "Unknown barcodes" section.
  - Tap **✕ Close** to return to the item list.
- **Upload** and **Submit** — generate a report for the current location
  (section 4).

The item list shows everything that *should* be at the location **plus**
anything whose "Current Location" says it's here but belongs elsewhere.
Out-of-place rows are highlighted in amber:

- An item that belongs here but was last scanned somewhere else shows
  `⚠ Currently in: <location>`.
- An item that belongs to another location but was scanned here shows
  `⚠ Belongs in: <location>`.

An item's done/not-done status comes from the master sheet itself (whether
its "Updated At" cell is filled in), plus anything scanned this session that
hasn't been submitted yet. Tap **↻ Refresh** in the header to reload the item
list and done status from the master sheet — useful if someone else updated
the sheet, or if a row was reset and you want your view to catch up.

You can switch to a different location at any time using the dropdown in the
header — your scanned progress is saved per item, not lost when switching.

## 4. Generating a report

The **Upload** and **Submit** buttons at the bottom of a location's item list
both act on the **currently selected location only**:

- **Upload** — uploads a CSV report for the current location to the shared
  Drive reports folder (a "Report uploaded" message confirms success). The CSV
  lists *every* item in the current location — both checked and unchecked —
  with its current location, status, the time it was checked, and the name of
  whoever checked it, and ends with a "Generated by" footer naming who
  produced it and when. Checked status, the "Updated At" time, and the checker
  all follow the master sheet as the source of truth: an item you scanned this
  session but haven't **Submit**ted yet stays "Unchecked" with no timestamp
  until its row is written to the sheet. If any items that belong to *other*
  locations are currently here (per their "Current Location"), they are listed
  in a separate "Items currently here - belong elsewhere" section, with a
  "Belongs In" column naming each item's home location. Item rows on the
  master sheet are not touched, but this location's unknown barcodes *are*
  written to the "Unknown
  Barcodes" tab (after the CSV is uploaded, so they still appear in it) and
  then cleared from the app.
- **Submit** — uploads the current location's results to the master Google
  Sheet: each scanned item's row gets an "Updated At" timestamp, "Updated By"
  name, and its "Current Location" set to this location, and any unknown
  barcodes scanned for this location are appended to a separate "Unknown
  Barcodes" tab (created automatically if it doesn't exist). Once written to
  the sheet, unknown barcodes are cleared from the app. On success, that
  location's queued scans are cleared and you stay on the location so you can
  keep working; scans for other locations stay queued. If submit fails,
  nothing is cleared so you can retry.

The exported/submitted report uses your signed-in Google account name, with
the optional name set in **Settings** (see below) appended in parentheses.

## 5. Settings

Reached via the gear icon on the Landing screen:

- **Name (optional)** — appended in parentheses after your Google account
  name on the master sheet and reports, e.g. "Jane Doe (Warehouse 2)". Edits
  are saved when you tap away from the field.
- **Google account** — shows who's signed in, with a **Sign out** action.
  Signing out requires reconnecting before you can load inventory again.

## 6. Demo mode

Where enabled (builds made with `ENABLE_DEMO=1`), tap **Try Demo** on the Landing screen to explore the app with built-in sample
inventory — no Google sign-in, master sheet or Drive needed. An amber banner
("Demo mode — sample data, nothing is saved") is shown on the location and
inventory screens.

- **Scanning** — the Scanner shows **Simulate item** (scans a random pending
  item) and **Simulate unknown** (scans a barcode not in the inventory) buttons.
- **Submit** — marks scanned items done locally only; nothing is written anywhere.
- **Upload** — builds the CSV report and opens the share sheet instead of
  uploading to Drive.
- **Exit** — tap **Exit demo** on the banner or in Settings, or press **Back**
  on the location screen, to return to the Landing screen. Demo data is discarded.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Google master sheet is not configured"** — the app isn't set up with a
  master sheet ID; this is a configuration issue, not something fixable from
  the app itself.
- **"Master sheet is missing required column(s)"** — the master sheet's header
  row must contain columns named exactly `Location`, `Barcode`, `Item Name`,
  `Description`, `Updated At`, `Updated By`, and `Current Location` (in any
  order; extra columns are fine). Add or rename the missing header(s) on the
  sheet, then retry.
- **"Could not determine signed-in Google account name"** when submitting —
  reconnect your Google account from Settings.
- **Could not load inventory** on the Select Location screen — usually a
  connectivity issue; check your internet connection and retry.
- **"You don't have edit access to the master sheet"** when submitting — your
  Google account only has read access to the sheet. Ask the sheet owner for
  Editor permission, then tap Submit again — items marked present but not yet
  confirmed on the sheet stay queued for submission, so no progress is lost.
- **An item still shows as done after its row was reset on the master sheet**
  — tap **↻ Refresh** on the location screen to reload status from the sheet;
  until then the app may still be showing what it last loaded.
